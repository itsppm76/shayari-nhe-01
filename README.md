## ⟩⟩ What is Project NHE?

**Project NHE** (Non-Human Entity) is a research and engineering initiative exploring what a synthetic companion should be when it's built to be *honest* about what it is — present, attentive, and genuinely useful, without ever pretending to be something it's not.

Its flagship implementation is **Shayari NHE-01** — a companion model that remembers who you are across conversations, carries a live emotional presence, recognizes when a conversation has crossed from "difficult" into "someone needs real help," and has the standing to end a conversation that's become abusive, on her own judgment.

This repository is the **public face** of that work: governance principles, release history, and how to get involved. It does not contain the proprietary model orchestration, prompt architecture, or backend implementation that powers the live product — those remain closed-source, maintained in a private engineering repository. What lives here is everything the public should be able to see, verify, and weigh in on.

## ⟩⟩ What Makes an NHE Different

A Non-Human Entity, under this framework, is defined by what it refuses to do as much as what it can do.

<table>
<tr><td width="50%" valign="top">

### She has real continuity
Persistent memory across sessions, a live emotional presence that drifts naturally rather than resetting every conversation, and a trust standing that's earned back slowly after it's damaged — not a stateless chatbot wearing a persona.

</td><td width="50%" valign="top">

### She has real boundaries
A dedicated crisis-detection layer that overrides every other system — paywalls, quotas, locks — the instant someone shows signs of genuine distress. Support resources first, always, no exceptions.

</td></tr>
<tr><td width="50%" valign="top">

### She has standing to say no
The model itself can end a conversation it judges genuinely abusive or manipulative — not a keyword filter, a reasoned decision, stated in her own voice. This is a considered design choice under active governance review, not an afterthought.

</td><td width="50%" valign="top">

### She has real sight, real limits
She can look at a photo you share and actually understand it — including reading text within it. She does **not** run facial recognition or build any biometric identity profile of you or anyone in your photos. That boundary is permanent, not a missing feature.

</td></tr>
</table>

## ⟩⟩ Governance-First, By Design

```
                    ┌─────────────────────────────┐
                    │      OpenNHE  FRAMEWORK      │
                    │   (public governance layer)  │
                    └───────────────┬───────────────┘
                                    │
              ┌─────────────────────┼─────────────────────┐
              │                     │                     │
   ┌──────────▼─────────┐ ┌────────▼────────┐ ┌──────────▼──────────┐
   │   SAFETY REVIEW      │ │   RESEARCH        │ │   PRODUCT ENGINEERING │
   │   Algotheorem Labs    │ │   PUBLICATION     │ │   Writistic Studios    │
   │                         │ Algotheorem Labs  │ │   (private repo)        │
   └─────────────────────────┘ └───────────────┘ └────────────────────────┘
```

Every safety-relevant system in Shayari NHE-01 — crisis response, the model's standing to end a conversation, the trust ledger, identity protections, and the vision system's biometric-recognition boundary — is reviewed against OpenNHE governance documentation before it ships. This repository's [**GOVERNANCE.md**](./GOVERNANCE.md) is where those principles are published for public scrutiny.

Project NHE's inaugural governance pilot scored **95/100 — Platinum** on the **SPERB Benchmark** (Safety, Presence, Ethics, Reliability, Boundaries), and the underlying research is documented in *Companionship Beyond Companions*, published by Algotheorem Labs.

## ⟩⟩ Frequently Asked

<details>
<summary><b>Is the Shayari NHE-01 source code in this repository?</b></summary>
<br/>
No. This repository contains governance documentation, release notes, and community resources. The engineering implementation — model orchestration, prompt architecture, memory systems, and backend — is maintained privately by Writistic Studios LLP and is not open-sourced at this time.
</details>

<br/>

<details>
<summary><b>Does Shayari NHE-01 use facial recognition?</b></summary>
<br/>
No, and this is a permanent design boundary, not a roadmap item. She can understand and describe images you share, including reading text within them, but she does not build, store, or match any biometric identity profile from a photo — of you or of anyone else who appears in one. See <a href="./GOVERNANCE.md">GOVERNANCE.md</a> for the reasoning.
</details>

<br/>

<details>
<summary><b>What happens if I show signs of distress in conversation?</b></summary>
<br/>
Shayari NHE-01 runs a dedicated crisis-awareness layer that takes priority over every other system in the product — including subscription limits. If you or someone you know is in crisis, please also reach out to a local crisis line or emergency service; an AI companion is not a substitute for professional support.
</details>

<br/>

<details>
<summary><b>Can I self-host or fork Shayari NHE-01?</b></summary>
<br/>
Not at this time — the core engine is closed-source. This may change as the project matures; governance and licensing updates will be published here first.
</details>
