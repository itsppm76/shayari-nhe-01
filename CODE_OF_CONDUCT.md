## ⟩ Our Pledge

We, as contributors and maintainers of Project NHE, pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

Given this project's subject matter — a companion AI designed with real emotional presence and safety-critical crisis awareness — we hold ourselves to a particularly high standard around discussions of mental health, vulnerability, and wellbeing. This is a space for serious, good-faith engagement with hard topics, not a space for exploiting them.

<br/>

## ⟩ Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**
- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes, and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the project as a whole

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**
- Sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Attempting to use discussions about crisis-response or safety systems to solicit, share, or normalize self-harm
- Any conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

<br/>

## ⟩ A Note on Sensitive Topics

This project involves genuine research into crisis detection and emotional support. Discussions here may reference difficult subjects — mental health, self-harm, distress — in a research or product-design context. If you are personally struggling, this repository is not a support channel; please reach out to a local crisis line or emergency service. We'll always point toward real help rather than attempt to substitute for it here.

<br/>

## ⟩ Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior deemed inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when appropriate.

<br/>

## ⟩ Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces associated with this repository — issues, discussions, pull requests — and also applies when an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

<br/>

## ⟩ Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via the process described in [SECURITY.md](./SECURITY.md) (for anything requiring privacy) or by opening a report through GitHub's reporting tools. All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the project's leadership.

<br/>

## ⟩ Enforcement Guidelines

| Level | Community Impact | Consequence |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Correction** | Use of inappropriate language or other unprofessional behavior | Private, written warning; clarification of why the behavior was inappropriate |
| **2. Warning** | A violation through a single incident or series of actions | A warning with consequences for continued behavior; no interaction with those involved for a specified period |
| **3. Temporary Ban** | A serious violation of community standards | Temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the project for a specified period |
| **4. Permanent Ban** | Demonstrating a pattern of violation, including sustained harassment | Permanent ban from any public interaction within the project |
